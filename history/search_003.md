**Research Notes: The Dark Reality of Mother Teresa**

**1. What is known / consensus:**

Mother Teresa was a Catholic nun and missionary who founded the Missionaries of Charity. She was widely revered for her charitable work, particularly in establishing orphanages, clinics, and other institutions to care for the poor and marginalized. Her reputation as a symbol of compassion and sacrifice was widely recognized globally.

**2. What is recent or newly discovered:**

Recent allegations and investigations have revealed a darker side to Mother Teresa's work and legacy. These include:
- Secret baptisms of dying Hindus and Muslims without their permission (as reported in [1] and echoed in Leo Massberg's biography of Mother Teresa).
- Denial of painkillers to cancer patients and reuse of unsterilized needles in clinics.
- Denial of adoptions to keep children in orphanages for longer periods.
- Association with dictators, fraudsters, and Catholic sex offenders.

**3. What is contested or uncertain:**

While the allegations mentioned above have been reported in various sources, including [1], it is essential to note that the accuracy and extent of these claims may be subject to debate and further investigation. Some of the allegations may be based on individual accounts or anecdotal evidence, which may not be representative of the broader organization or Mother Teresa's overall work. Additionally, the motivations and intentions behind these actions, if they did occur, are unclear and may be subject to interpretation.
